# Prompts de imagem (9:16) — use no Midjourney/Flux/Ideogram

Estilo base (cole em todos): `dark noir illustration, cinematic, high contrast, deep shadows, red accent light, film grain, vertical 9:16, no text, no watermark`

Evite pedir rostos de atores reais: use silhuetas, mãos, costas e objetos para manter o estilo e reduzir risco de direitos de imagem.

1. **Gancho** — silhouette of a man in a three-piece suit seen from behind on an empty theater stage, single golden spotlight, floating dust
2. **O erro** — old CRT television glowing red in a dark room, static, a faint red smiley face drawn in blood-red paint on the wall behind it
3. **A virada** — empty dark stage, cold blue light, an overturned chair, silence
4. **A família** — warm amber-lit family house window at night, two small silhouettes inside, then second image: showman silhouette bowing to a crying crowd
5. **Sem CBI** — investigation wall with photos and red strings fading to blank, stamped folder "ARQUIVADO" (add text in editor, not in the prompt)
6. **Red John livre** — huge red smiley face glowing behind a half-open door in a dark corridor, a long shadow on the floor
7. **Fecho** — bathroom mirror, man's face half lit and half in shadow, a red smiley faintly visible in the reflection
